## ⏸ Cutter `Output full`

Deliberately deferred.

Reason: Farmer's Delight Cutting may have chance outputs. Jade must not call `rollResults()` merely to inspect the Cutter. Do not add this line until a deterministic non-RNG capacity probe is designed.

## 🧹 Legacy provider cleanup

The obsolete `FarmerKnifeJadeProvider.java` implementation has been reduced to a non-provider compatibility tombstone in the completion patch.

Keep the live provider:

```text
FarmerHarvestToolJadeProvider
UID = easyfarmersdelightcompat:farmer_knife
```

The historical UID is intentional so Jade preferences do not reset.
